What is sent, and what never is

Only for page elements that already look like ads, a short structural summary goes to the Semantic Blocker service. That's the element's position and size category, what kind of links it has, whether it shows a disclosure label like "Sponsored", and up to 280 characters of its visible text with emails, card numbers, phone numbers and links removed.

Never sent: page addresses, HTML, form fields or anything you type, cookies, or anything from banking, email, health, password-manager or local-network sites. The service returns probabilities only. The extension itself decides what to hide.

Off: semantic detection runs a local heuristic only, and nothing about the pages you visit leaves your browser.
