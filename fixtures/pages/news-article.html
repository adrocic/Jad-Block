City council approves new transit plan

The city council voted seven to two on Tuesday night to approve a transit plan that adds three bus rapid transit corridors and extends light rail service into the eastern suburbs.

Supporters said the plan would cut average commute times, while opponents questioned the projected ridership figures and the long-term cost of maintaining the new stations.

Construction on the first corridor is expected to begin next spring, according to the city's transportation department, with service starting within three years.

The public hearing drew hundreds of residents, and the community festival that followed was sponsored by local businesses who wanted to celebrate the long-awaited decision.
